    #### 1. Universal Dynamic Stock Resolution (Zero Hardcoding)
  • Problem: Research previously only supported candidate seed stocks, returning 404 or empty states for unseeded tickers.

  • Solution: Implemented dynamic exchange lookup in [resolver.py](file:///d:/Documents/enam%20assignment/apps/api/finn/db/resolver.py). When a user searches for any listed stock (e.g. TATAMOTORS, WIPRO, SBIN, ITC), the system queries live market providers to resolve official legal names, ISINs, sectors, and exchange ticker symbols (.NS / .BO), registering them dynamically into the securities master.


    • #### 2. Restored Deep 8-Module Research Desk
  • Problem: Previous commit had wiped fundamentals.py and set modules: None, breaking the research analysis view and hiding all deep company profiles.

  • Solution: Re-architected [engine.py](file:///d:/Documents/enam%20assignment/apps/api/finn/research/engine.py) to fetch live financial disclosures and construct the full 8 research modules on demand:

    • 1. Business Snapshot: Core solutions, segment revenue shares, geographical exposure.
    2. Financial Snapshot: Revenue growth (YoY & QoQ), operating margins, net profit (PAT), free cash flow (FCF), trailing P/E, ROE, and DSO.
    3. Trajectory Analysis: Topline expansion vectors and structural margin drags.
    4. Balance Sheet Health: Net cash vs debt stance, leverage ratios, and working capital cycles.
    5. Cash Flow Quality: Operating Cash Flow (OCF) vs FCF conversion ratios and earnings quality verdict.
    6. Guidance & Narrative vs Numbers: Quarter-by-quarter management assertions juxtaposed against actual reported metrics.
    7. Bull vs Bear Thesis: Structural compounding drivers versus downside risks.
    8. Primary Sources: Official verifiable filings, quarterly results, earnings concalls, and annual reports.

    What Was Built


  • Push Mode (Portfolio Pulse):

    • - Windowed Digest: Filtered, prioritized corporate disclosures across flexible windows (1h, 6h, 24h, 3d, 7d, custom date picker) with automatic recent fallback so screens never stay blank.

    Key Decisions & Trade-Offs


  • Primary sources over secondary commentary: Grounded strictly in official issuer disclosures, audited statements, and concall transcripts. If evidence is missing, the system returns an honest limitation rather than fabricating an answer.

  • Single-container architecture: Bundled the React/Vite SPA and Python/FastAPI engine into a multi-stage Docker build for reproducible zero-config deployment.
